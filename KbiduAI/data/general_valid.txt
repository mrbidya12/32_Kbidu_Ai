Supervised learning is a type of machine learning where models are trained on labeled data, learning to map inputs to outputs.

Unsupervised learning is a type of machine learning that uses unlabeled data to discover hidden patterns or data groupings.

Semi-supervised learning uses both labeled and unlabeled data to train machine learning models, combining the strengths of supervised and unsupervised learning.

Reinforcement learning involves training agents to make sequences of decisions by rewarding desirable behaviors and punishing undesirable ones.

Natural language understanding (NLU) is a subset of NLP that focuses on machine comprehension of human language, enabling systems to grasp meaning, intent, and context.

Natural language generation (NLG) is a subset of NLP that focuses on generating natural language text or speech from structured data.

Sentiment analysis uses NLP to identify and extract subjective information from source materials, determining the emotional tone behind words.

Machine translation is the task of automatically translating text from one language to another using computational methods.

Text summarization is the process of creating a concise and coherent summary of a longer text document.

Information retrieval is the process of obtaining relevant information from a collection of resources based on a user's query.

Question answering systems automatically answer questions posed by humans in natural language.

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is a subtask of NLP that locates and classifies named entities in text into predefined categories such as person names, organizations, locations, and more.

Coreference resolution is the task of finding all expressions that refer to the same entity in a text.

Dependency parsing is the process of analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence to establish relationships between words.

Part-of-speech tagging is the process of assigning a part-of-speech label (noun, verb, adjective, etc.) to each word in a text.

Semantic similarity measures how similar two pieces of text are in terms of their meaning, which is crucial for many NLP applications.

Text classification is the task of assigning predefined categories to text documents.

Topic modeling is a technique for discovering abstract topics in a collection of documents.
